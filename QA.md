## Layout / interaction checks
- No horizontal overflow at 1440 or 390; no clipped text (only visually-hidden .sr helpers report overflow).
- Touch targets: every visible link/button/summary is at least 44x44 (filter radios are 44px-high labels).
- Focus: 3px navy outline (cream on dark bands) via :focus-visible; B filter chips show a ring on the visible pill when the hidden radio is focused; whole-card focus ring in B.
- B filter: radio group in a fieldset/legend, arrow keys move selection, aria-live status text ("Showing 2 of 3 attorneys: Premises"), empty state with reset. Verified results for every chip.
- C: roster links switch the panel, move focus to the name heading, support #hash deep links (verified), mobile scrolls to the panel.
- No-JS: reveals visible, no hidden panels (C stacks all three profiles), B grid shows all cards with the filter hidden, mobile menu falls back to an always-visible link list.
- prefers-reduced-motion: transforms removed, reveals become a 150 ms opacity fade, hero animation off, card lift/image scale off.
- Motion: opacity and transform only (header shadow is a pre-rendered pseudo-element whose opacity animates). Hover transitions 200–300 ms. Reveal 500 ms, 80 ms stagger, plays once, 15% threshold.
- Fonts: Fraunces and Public Sans (SIL OFL) subset and embedded in fonts/ (relative path); falls back to Georgia / system-ui.

## Known limitations
- Tested in headless Chrome only; Safari/Firefox and real devices not tested.
- Screen-reader behaviour not tested by hand.
- Monogram portraits are placeholders; real photography needs a re-check of crop and contrast.
